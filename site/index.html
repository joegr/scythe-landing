Cut cards. Keep the spans.

Scythe is a debate card cutter that is also a spaCy labeling tool. Underline, highlight and emphasis are stored as token-aligned spans, so every card you cut is training data too. Cards live as issues in a GitHub repository you own.

One card, two views

Formatting a card should not destroy the record of what was cut.

Every card is cut verbatim from the source, then tokenized exactly once. The underlines, highlights and emphasis you add are stored against those tokens, not as loose styling, so nothing drifts when the card is printed, exported or re-read.

"spans": {
  "underline": [[4, 8], [13, 17], [19, 25], [29, 34], [38, 45]],
  "highlight": [[14, 17], [20, 24], [24, 25], [38, 40]],
  "emphasis":  [[24, 25]]
}

What it does

  • Reader mode. Paste or open a source, select a passage, cut it. The text stays verbatim and the cite is filled in.
  • Lossless spans. Formatting round-trips through spaCy DocBin, Doc.to_json() and Prodigy JSONL.
  • Your repo, your cards. Each card is a GitHub issue; its source is the parent issue. Backlinks, labels and history come free.
  • A searchable graph. Sources, cards, authors and entities link up, filed under 542 past NSDA topics or your own.
  • Prints clean. One canonical Markdown drives the view, the export and the printed page.
